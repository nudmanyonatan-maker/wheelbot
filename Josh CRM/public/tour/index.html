The whole job, A to Z

Each step is what happens in the business, and what you do in ISO Desk when it does.

1
A shop starts working with youAdd it once, with its people.

What happens

A broker shop (an ISO) signs our ISO agreement and starts sending merchants.

In ISO Desk

  • Shops, Add shop. It warns you if the shop is already here under another spelling.
  • Add the owner, the reps who send deals and whoever handles files. Each can have two numbers.
  • Mark the ISO agreement signed and the W-9 received. Commission cannot be paid without them.
  • Write a note on how they work: what they send, who to call, how they like to be reached.
  • Duplicate shops show a Merge button. Nothing is lost when you merge.
2
Keep the shop sending dealsFollow-ups, calls and texts.

What happens

Shops send more to the funders who stay in touch. A shop that goes quiet is usually sending to someone else.

In ISO Desk

  • Today lists shops that have gone quiet (no deal in 30 days, you can change it in Settings).
  • Follow-up, top right, works from anywhere: who, when, what for. It can repeat every week.
  • When one comes due it pops up on your screen, even with the desk closed, with Call, Text, Done and Later.
  • Call opens Connect UC with the number ready. When you hang up, click Hung up, write what happened and pick the next follow-up in the same box. Saving it closes the follow-up you called about.
  • Text uses your templates, which fill in the name, merchant and what is missing. Click Copy text, paste it into Connect UC and send. The desk keeps a copy.
  • Each shop shows its last 90 days: how many deals sent, approved and funded, and the most common decline reason.
3
A deal comes inLog it, and check who sent it first.

What happens

The shop sends a merchant's application and the last three months of bank statements.

In ISO Desk

  • Deals, Log a submission. Pick the shop and who sent it, then the merchant's details.
  • If another shop already sent that merchant, it tells you who got there first and when.
  • Tick what came with it. Anything missing shows on the card and on Today.
  • Text the shop for it shows the exact documents still missing.
4
Underwriting decidesApprove with offers, or decline with a reason.

What happens

Underwriting approves with one or more offers, or declines.

In ISO Desk

  • Send to underwriting once the file is complete. It will not move until the paperwork is in.
  • Record an approval: amount, buy rate, term, payments, max points, fee.
  • Record a decline: pick the reason, then text the shop why in one tap.
5
The offer goes outPoints, payments and the shop's commission.

What happens

The shop adds its points and presents the offer. The merchant picks one.

In ISO Desk

  • Slide the points to see the merchant's payment, payback, estimated APR and what the shop earns.
  • Press Merchant picked this. The deal moves to Contracts out.
  • Today flags an offer the shop has not answered after 2 days.
6
Contracts and fundingEverything in before it funds.

What happens

Contract signed, documents verified, a funding call, then the money goes out.

In ISO Desk

  • The checklist: contract signed, voided check, driver's license, proof of ownership, bank login verified, funding call.
  • In CA, CT, FL, GA, KS, MO, NY, UT and VA it adds the cost disclosure the law requires.
  • Mark funded once it is all in. It will not let you before.
7
After it fundsCommission, payback and the renewal.

What happens

The shop gets its commission. The merchant pays back daily or weekly and can usually renew at half paid.

In ISO Desk

  • Mark the shop's commission paid, so you can answer them when they ask.
  • See how much is paid back and the date it reaches half.
  • At half paid it shows on Today. Start renewal makes a new deal under the same shop.
8
Your dataImport, download and backups.

What happens

Your shops and history are yours, and they move with you.

In ISO Desk

  • Import takes phone contacts, a SmartMCA or Excel export saved as CSV, or a backup.
  • Settings, Your data: download your shops and people, your deals, or a full backup any time.
  • Everything saves to your account and stays in sync on every device. If your connection drops, changes wait on the device and save when you are back online.

Words you will see

The same words the desk uses on screen.

ISO, or shopA broker shop that sends us merchants.
Factor rateWhat the merchant pays back per dollar. 1.35 on $10,000 is $13,500 back.
Buy rate and pointsOur price to the shop, plus what the shop adds. Each point adds 0.01 to the factor and pays the shop 1% of the advance.
Open positionsOther cash advances the merchant is still paying back.
RenewalA new advance for the same merchant, usually once half the last one is paid back.
Gone quietA shop with no new deal in 30 days.
